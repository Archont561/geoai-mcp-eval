## What this page will contain

- A one-paragraph answer to the main question, licensed only by the RQ1 gate outcome,
  in neutral language.
- The three-leg claim, updated to whatever survived the data: **instrument** (the
  frozen harness: tasks-as-YAML, predicate oracle, results contract) · **estimate, not
  declaration** (effect size + intervals at a stated power limit) · **mechanism**
  (enforcement depth N1–N4 vs protocol identity).
- Limits: what N, seeds and provider drift permit claiming; what would falsify the
  mechanism reading.
- What the instrument enables next: the scaled study (N ≥ 80) and the re-scoping rule
  if stronger counter-evidence appears.

Claims will cite only the corpus bibliography; no new sources are introduced at summary
time.
