# Decision: Scoring contract (ISO 19157-derived predicates)

## Status

Proposed (to freeze at v1.0-prereg)

## Decision

Scored on executed artefacts via ISO 19157-derived predicates, tiers A-D:

- Tier A: existence (does the artefact exist?)
- Tier B: spatial predicates (topological correctness)
- Tier C: value-level vs baseline
- Tier D: repeatability/economics

Scoring contract (WSZ/OQS definitions, weights, tie handling) is defined in the
frozen specification §§6.7–6.8 and intentionally not restated in this repository.

## Rationale

From `docs/study/experiment.md`: scoring and analysis are by reference to the
frozen specification. Preregistration → Załącznik E (outside this repository).
